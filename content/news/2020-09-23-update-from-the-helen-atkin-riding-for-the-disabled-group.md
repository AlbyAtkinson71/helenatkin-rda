We had hoped to start back riding this term but the rules and regulations set out by RDA National as well as the Covid-19 risk assessment of the activities, has made it logistically difficult to keep riders and volunteers safe. 

We are therefore looking to start back in the spring, when we will have a roofed arena. Can we reassure you that we really appreciate your continued support and willingness to volunteer. 

There are various online activities that riders can at home to keep their interest going. Please look at the [RDA National website](https://www.rda.org.uk/) for more information.
