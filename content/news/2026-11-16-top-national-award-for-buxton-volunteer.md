Buxton volunteer Janette Sykes has earned national recognition for almost 40 years' service to a High Peak charity providing life changing therapy for disabled people of all ages.

Janette is one of 18 long-serving colleagues across the UK to receive the prestigious President's Award 2026 – one of the highest distinctions awarded by the national Riding for the Disabled Association (RDA) – for decades of dedication to Helen Atkin Group RDA.

She received the coveted award from leading equestrian expert Jane Holderness-Roddam CVO CBE, lady-in-waiting to RDA President The Princess Royal, at a special ceremony during the RDA's Annual Gathering in Coventry on Friday November 13.

"I feel both honoured and privileged to have received this award from our national RDA," said Janette, who first became actively involved in disabled riding groups in north east Derbyshire in 1987 before moving to Buxton two years later.

"I also feel very fortunate that I have been able to share my life-long love of horses and horse riding, enthusiasm and experience with so many riders for almost 40 years – most of them with equally dedicated and committed colleagues at Helen Atkin Group RDA in Buxton.

"It has been a huge pleasure and privilege to contribute to the inspirational and invaluable work of RDA for so long, and to have played a pro-active role in the positive and life-enriching impact it can have on disabled people of all backgrounds, abilities and ages.

"It's been so rewarding for me to watch both riders and volunteers develop and extend their physical and communication skills in a community-focused and caring environment – especially when they achieve much more than they think they can. And in recent times, it's been very satisfying to welcome younger volunteers into the team to take the group forward into an even brighter future."

Over the years, Janette has served as a Trustee; has used her professional journalistic and PR skills to promote the group locally, regionally and nationally; has played a key role in fundraising; has assisted riders in regional and national competitions; and still enjoys giving hands-on help during the group's regular riding sessions in the group's covered arena at Buxton Riding School, Fern Road.

Janette was nominated by Helen Atkin Group Chairperson Janine Frost, who said: "We are all thrilled that Janette has received this prestigious accolade for all that she has done and continues to do for our RDA group and our riders. She is a very dedicated colleague and is very generous with her time, supporting riders during our weekly sessions and working tirelessly doing PR on our behalf.

"She is also a major fundraiser. Last year she and her husband Colin – also a volunteer with RDA – raised £3000 for a public defibrillator at our covered arena by donating all the money they raised over just six months, singing and playing music together as performance duo Take Two. We consider ourselves very fortunate to have her support."

Helen Atkin Group RDA is a voluntary group (charity number 1182183) providing riding therapy for disabled children and adults aged from six to over 60 across the High Peak.
